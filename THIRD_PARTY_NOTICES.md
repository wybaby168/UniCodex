# Third-party notices

## Codex model metadata

`Resources/CodexModels.json` is a filtered snapshot of the public model catalog from [OpenAI Codex](https://github.com/openai/codex). The selected entries retain their upstream contents. The snapshot and its license are documented in [CodexCatalog-SOURCE.txt](Resources/CodexCatalog-SOURCE.txt) and [CodexCatalog-LICENSE.txt](Resources/CodexCatalog-LICENSE.txt).

UniCodex starts a separately installed Codex executable or obtains a verified engine from OpenAI’s release directory. The executable is distributed by its publisher.
